# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Ambassador Referral Linker** browser extension.

## Data Collection and Usage

The Ambassador Referral Linker extension is designed with your privacy in mind. We are transparent about what data is handled and how it is used.

- **Contributor ID:** The Contributor ID is explicitly provided by you, the user.
- **Local & Synced Storage:** Your Contributor ID and extension preferences are stored using `chrome.storage.sync` (or the equivalent browser sync storage). Depending on your browser settings, this data may be synchronized by your browser (e.g., Google Chrome) across your devices.
- **No Developer Access:** The developer of this extension does not collect, receive, or have any access to your Contributor ID or any other personal data.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** This extension does not use any analytics software and does not track your behavior.
- **No Backend Server:** There are no external servers or backends associated with this extension. All operations happen locally within your browser.
- **No Browsing History Collection:** We do not collect, monitor, or store your browsing history. 
- **Microsoft Links:** The extension's sole purpose is to insert your Contributor ID into Microsoft links (via the `wt.mc_id` parameter). When you access or share these modified links, the identifier is naturally sent to and received by Microsoft as part of the URL.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

- **No Sale of Data:** We do not sell your personal data.
- **No Sharing for Advertising:** We do not share your data with any third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Compliance

Our data usage fully complies with the **Chrome Web Store User Data Policy**, including the **Limited Use** requirements. The extension only utilizes permissions necessary for its core functionality—modifying URLs to append your Contributor ID—and does not use your data for any other purposes.
